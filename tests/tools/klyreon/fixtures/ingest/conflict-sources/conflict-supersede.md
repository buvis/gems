# Source: certainty is reachable, and the earlier framing was wrong

<!-- stub-marker: conflict-supersede -->

Enough converging evidence makes a claim certain. The earlier framing that
tied certainty to a bare count of sources was wrong: it is the independence of
the lines, not their number, that does the work. This replaces the earlier
claim outright.
