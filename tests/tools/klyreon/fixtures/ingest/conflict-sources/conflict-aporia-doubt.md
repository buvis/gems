# Source: certainty is a psychological state, not an evidential one

<!-- stub-marker: conflict-aporia-doubt -->

Certainty is a feeling about a claim, not a property evidence can confer. No
amount of evidence crosses a claim from probable to certain; the crossing
happens in the knower, not in the warrant. Both positions are defensible and
neither refutes the other.
