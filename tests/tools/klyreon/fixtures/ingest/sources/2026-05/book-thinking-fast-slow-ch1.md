# Thinking, Fast and Slow: chapter 1

<!-- stub-marker: book-thinking-fast-slow-ch1 -->

The mind runs two systems. System 1 is fast, automatic, and effortless; it
answers before you decide to answer. System 2 is slow, deliberate, and
effortful; it is the one you mean when you say "I". Most of the time System 1
runs the show and System 2 endorses its impressions without checking them.
Errors arise when a System 1 answer is confidently wrong and System 2 is too
lazy to override it.
